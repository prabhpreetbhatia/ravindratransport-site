What we do

One firm for your road transport: the right vehicle for the load, a verified driver, and a team that tracks every trip.

Open lorries

Full truckloads from 7 to 35 tons, on 4- to 16-wheel lorries, for industrial and agricultural goods.

32-foot containers

Closed SXL and MXL containers for bulky goods that need protection from weather and moisture.

Trailers

40-foot trailers on 12 to 18 wheels, including low-bed and semi-low-bed, available on demand for heavy machinery and project cargo.

GPS tracking

We track every vehicle on the road and keep you told where your goods are, so the receiver can plan.

End-to-end transport

We can handle the whole move, from pickup at your plant to delivery at your customer's door.

About us

Mr. Dilip Singh Ji Bhatia founded the firm in Pune in 1972. Ravindra Singh Bhatia, an electronics engineer, joined him in 1992 and runs the business today.

We started by serving industries around Pune and Satara and now carry loads across India. Our clients come from pharmaceuticals, poultry, food, automotive, thermoplastics and manufacturing.

Common questions

If your question isn't here, call us.

How do I book a lorry?

Call or email us with the pickup and drop locations, the goods, their weight, and your date. We will reply with a vehicle and a quote.

Which vehicles do you have?

Open lorries from 7 to 35 tons, 32-foot closed containers, and 40-foot trailers. See the vehicle table above.

Can I track my goods?

Yes. We track every vehicle by GPS and keep you told where it is until delivery.

Are your drivers checked?

Yes. We check the background of every driver, and each one is licensed and insured.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer (NEFT / IMPS) to our company account, or by card. We send a proper invoice for every trip.
