Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ravindra Transport ("we", "us") runs road transport from Pune, Maharashtra, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and company name, when you call, email or message us.
  • Details of your shipment: pickup and drop addresses, goods, weight and dates.
  • Messages you send us by phone, email or WhatsApp.
  • For drivers and vehicle owners we work with: name, phone number, licence and vehicle details.

This website does not use forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To give you a quote, book a vehicle and deliver your goods.
  • To keep you told about your trip, including by WhatsApp messages from our business number.
  • To send invoices and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

We share only what a trip needs: the driver or vehicle owner carrying your goods, and the person receiving them. We use service providers such as WhatsApp (Meta) to send messages. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep trip and invoice records as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, and other information only as long as we need it to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you WhatsApp messages (reply STOP at any time). Contact us at the details below.
